            Campaign

            Twenty chapters on four fronts, flown with three wingmen against four of theirs. Each chapter has its own sky and its own orders, and keeps up to three stars. Ten stars on a front open the next.

            0 / 60 stars

            Team dogfight

            Four against four, five minutes, air start. Bots fill the empty seats; pilots with the same squadron code fly on the same side. First side to 25 wins. Kills and wins count for the team dogfight ranking and pay requisition points.

            – pilots flying now

            Zone control

            Zone control: three rings in the sky. Fly inside a ring with more aircraft than the other side to take it; each pays a point every three seconds and a kill pays two. The airfield rearms and repairs you, the radar halves your wait to fly again, the harbour pays double. First side to 100 wins.

            – pilots flying now

            Carrier strike

            Carrier strike: each side has an aircraft carrier. Sink theirs with bombs and rockets before they sink yours. The carriers shoot back; fly low over your own carrier to rearm and repair.

            – pilots flying now

            Island assault

            Island assault: both sides have landed troops and tanks on the same island. Strafe their squads, break their tanks with rockets and bombs, and help your men take the three strongpoints. Every strongpoint held pays a point every three seconds. First side to 200 wins; fly low over your own beach to rearm. A tank destroyed earns three bombs, a squad two rockets, an aircraft four rockets and repairs.

            – pilots flying now

            Island campaign

            Island campaign: the island assault on a bigger island, with an airstrip on its south shore. Land on the strip and stand there for three seconds to take it. The strip pays like a strongpoint, rearms and repairs whoever of its side lands on it, and its side takes off from it instead of flying in. A bomb on the runway closes it for thirty seconds. Each side has a radar and a fuel dump behind its beach: bomb their radar and they lose you on their scope, burn their fuel and they wait longer to fly again. First side to 200 wins.
