## English

**App Store alt başlık (30):** WWII air combat: fly and fight

**Play kısa açıklama (80):** WWII air combat. Fly, fight and train your pilot across four theatres.

**App Store tanıtım metni (170):** Sixteen fighters, four sides, seven sorties. Train your pilot, fit out your aircraft and bring her home. Everything is earned in the cockpit.

**Tam açıklama:**

Take off from carriers and airstrips in Operation Windfall, a WWII air-combat game.

Fly sixteen historic fighters over the Pacific, the English Channel, the Mediterranean and the Baltic. Dogfight enemy patrols, strafe and bomb ground targets, then bring your aircraft home.

• Seven sorties across four theatres
• Four sides: United States, Royal Air Force, Soviet Union and captured Axis types, each with its own briefings and colours
• Surprise targets and radio calls in the middle of a sortie
• Pilot skill tree, aircraft fittings and a loadout to choose before every flight
• Aircraft paints, flying kit and a quartermaster's showcase in the hangar
• A campaign story, a proper briefing and a full debrief after every flight
• Live ranking and a shared sky: fly the same sortie with friends
• English, Türkçe and Deutsch

Everything is earned by flying. Skill points, requisition points and stars open new aircraft, fittings, kit and paints. No adverts and no purchases.

No account needed to play; a pilot name is enough to enter the ranking, and you can delete your pilot at any time from inside the game. An internet connection is required.

Take off. The sky is waiting.

Starte in Operation Windfall von Flugzeugträgern und Feldflugplätzen: ein Luftkampfspiel im Zweiten Weltkrieg.

Flieg sechzehn historische Jäger über dem Pazifik, dem Ärmelkanal, dem Mittelmeer und der Ostsee. Kurvenkämpfe gegen feindliche Patrouillen, Tiefangriffe und Bomben auf Bodenziele, und dann die Maschine nach Hause bringen.

• Sieben Einsätze auf vier Kriegsschauplätzen
• Vier Seiten: Vereinigte Staaten, Royal Air Force, Sowjetunion und erbeutete Typen der Achse, jede mit eigenen Einsatzbesprechungen und Farben
