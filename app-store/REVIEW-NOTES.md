2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Operation Windfall is an arcade air-combat game set in the Second World War, for players aged 12+ who enjoy flight action games. It offers short single-player sorties, a 20-chapter campaign and team matches against bots and other players. It is free, has no ads and no purchases.

3. HOW TO ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
No account, login or sample file is needed.
- Tap TAKE OFF on the menu to fly a sortie. Left thumb steers, buttons on the right fire guns, bombs and rockets.
- Hangar: choose aircraft, paint and upgrades, all earned by playing.
- Online (optional): enter any pilot name to join the ranking, the campaign and team matches. This creates an anonymous profile; no email or password is asked.
- "Delete my pilot" in the same online menu erases that profile and all its data at once.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Supabase (EU): anonymous sign-in, pilot name, scores, campaign progress.
- Cloudflare Workers: the real-time match server for team matches.
- No payment processor, no advertising or analytics SDK, no third-party login.
- No AI service is used while the app runs. The radio voice lines are pre-recorded audio files produced with a licensed text-to-speech tool and shipped inside the app.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app works the same in every region. It is available in English, Turkish and German.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable. The app is not in a regulated industry. All 3D models, sounds, music and text were made for this game. Aircraft are referred to by their historical type names only; no third-party logos, characters or licensed content are used. The story and all persons in it are fictional.
